Rasmus Krigström sang lead in Ringmasters, the Swedish quartet that won the Barbershop Harmony Society's international championship in 2012, the first champions from outside North America. Deke Sharon talks with him about why Sweden sings, the five ages of music, AI and the human voice, what directors can learn from Swedish choirs, singing barbershop on cruise ships, and the one lever he would pull to change singing.
