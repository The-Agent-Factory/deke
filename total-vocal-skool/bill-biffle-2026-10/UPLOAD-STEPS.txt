Bill Biffle has been a barbershopper for nearly fifty years. He judged Sound and Singing for more than two decades, directed Albuquerque's New MexiChords, and served as International President of the Barbershop Harmony Society in 2009-2010. Deke Sharon sits down with him to talk about a cappella and barbershop in the 1970s, why big chapters thrived back then, what barbershop did better, and the long tug of war between harmony and rhythm.
