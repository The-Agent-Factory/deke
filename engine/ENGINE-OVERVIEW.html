Deke Content Engine

A machine on your laptop that keeps Deke's accounts posting every week from his own videos, with no batch waiting on you. It emails you only when something breaks.

Why posting kept stopping

Every past batch waited for a manual go. When the go did not come, posting stopped: in July, again in early October, and now. The last scheduled post went out Oct 25. When posts do go out, the community grows: Skool went from 215 to 302 members during the two weeks the October batch ran, and grew by 4 in the dark week before it.

The weekly loop

SUN 7:00FindChecks Deke's YouTube for new uploads.
SUNRefillIf under 6 weeks of clips, transcribes the next video and picks new clips.
SUNRenderVertical reels with gold captions and the Join Total Vocal end card.
SUNScheduleFills the next two weeks in Postiz, 7+ days ahead, and emails you the list.
DAILY 8:30WatchEmails you only if the queue runs low or a job fails.

Each week: 3 reels on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and TikTok, plus 2 text posts on Facebook, Threads and LinkedIn. That is the same shape as the October week that grew the community. To stop any post, delete it in Postiz before its time.

Guardrails

  • Every reel is a clip of Deke speaking in one of his published videos. Every text post is built from his own words, and the source and timestamp are recorded.
  • Anything with a guest speaking, a named third party or a minor is held for you and never posts on its own.
  • A checker rejects clips that cut through a word, run outside 12 to 60 seconds, say "choir directors" or use dashes.
  • Every post is pinned to Deke's own Postiz channels, so nothing can land on another account.

What only you can do

1. Switch on the schedule, once.Right-click claude_projects\deke\engine\install-tasks.ps1 and choose Run with PowerShell. It registers the Sunday run and the daily check. After that, the engine runs without you.
2. Send the link to Deke's backlog folder.His new Too Many Notes takes, the quick hits and the Robert Plant footage are not in the shared Dropbox, Google Drive, Gmail or on the laptop. He probably sent the link by WhatsApp. Paste it to Claude and the engine will add them.
