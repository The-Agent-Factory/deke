What you control

The on/off switchOff now. Say "go live" or "pause the engine" in any Claude session in the deke folder.
Any single postDelete it in Postiz before its time. Nothing re-sends it.
The dry runRun engine\dryrun.ps1. It pauses before each step: Enter runs it, S skips it, Q stops.
The scheduleWindows Task Scheduler holds three tasks named "Deke Engine". Disable any of them there.
